# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in CineHub a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity
and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Positive behavior includes using welcoming language, being respectful of
differing viewpoints, gracefully accepting constructive criticism, and focusing
on what is best for the community. Unacceptable behavior includes harassment,
trolling, insulting comments, and publishing others' private information.

## Enforcement

Instances of unacceptable behavior may be reported to the project maintainers
via a GitHub issue or discussion. Maintainers will review and take appropriate
action, up to and including temporary or permanent bans.
